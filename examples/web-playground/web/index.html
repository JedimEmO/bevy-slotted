      controls

      chest

      This scene has no page controls on purpose. Everything it shows is in the canvas, under the pointer.

      Sweep
      Hold the left button and drag across slots to split a carried stack evenly between them. Hold the right button instead and each slot takes one.
      Phantoms
      While a sweep is running the slots it will fill show a dimmed preview of what lands there. Let go and the previews become real; press Esc and they vanish.
      Tooltip
      Rest on an item for the theme’s hover delay, then hold Shift for the long form.
      browser

      Search is a grammar, not a substring match. Press a chip to type it into the browser’s field.

      Over a card: R opens its recipes, A bookmarks it, Enter tries the transfer.

      machine

      The furnace runs on menu properties the simulation writes. Redstone stops the cook without emptying it.

      The Sort button in the action rail was injected by the sorter mod, which has never seen this screen. The same mod puts it on the chest in the Mods scene.

      themes

      The screen tree does not change. Only the tokens and the materials do, and the open screen repaints in place.

      hud

      Layers are anchored to an edge, not to a screen. The hotbar comes from Rust; the clock comes from a mod.

      In edit mode every layer grows a handle. Drag it to a corner and the anchor follows.

      multiplayer

      Two clients and a server share this tab and one chest. The left client applies your click straight away and asks the server; the server answers, and a wrong guess is corrected in front of you.
